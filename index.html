A watch is the one thing you touch a hundred times a day.

Malak builds mechanical watches for people who notice details — cased in stainless steel, driven by automatic movements, and finished by hand in small batches.

View the collection Our story

Built to be worn, not admired from a drawer.

Every Malak case is water resistant to 100 metres and closed with a sapphire crystal, so the watch you wear diving is the same one you wear to dinner. The automatic movement inside runs on the motion of your wrist — no battery, no charging, just the wind-up energy of an ordinary day.

We keep the collection small on purpose. Three cases, a handful of dials, and a bracelet or strap for whichever one suits how you actually live.
