We started Malak because most watches are designed to be photographed, not worn.

A small workshop, not a factory

Malak began with a single case design and a question: how little can a watch need, and still be worth keeping for twenty years? We case our movements in-house, test every piece for water resistance by hand, and ship in batches small enough that someone checks each one before it leaves.

We don't chase trends in dial colour or case size. We'd rather make three watches well than thirty watches adequately.

Materials that earn their place

316L stainless steel for every case, sapphire crystal on every face, and automatic movements sourced from makers who've built them for decades. Straps are vegetable-tanned leather or waxed canvas — nothing that needs replacing after a summer.

If a part wears out, we'd rather repair it than sell you a new watch. Every Malak comes with parts and service support for as long as we're making watches.

What we hold to

Precision

Every movement is regulated to within a few seconds a day before it leaves the workshop, and re-checked after casing.

Restraint

We add a feature or a finish only if it earns its place on the wrist. Most things don't.

Provenance

You can trace every case, crystal, and movement in a Malak watch back to the workshop that made it.
